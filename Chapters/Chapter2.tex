\section{Functional Requirements \applicability{Development, Hybrid, Game; Research Software Prototype}}
\begin{guidance}
Specify observable system behaviour. Give each requirement a stable identifier
and write one testable statement using ``The system shall ...''. Do not describe
implementation details unless they are genuine constraints. Detailed acceptance
criteria and test cases belong in the software testing document. Follow a
consistent requirements-engineering convention such as ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148
where applicable~\citep{ieee29148}.
\end{guidance}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Functional requirements}
\label{tab:functional-requirements}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{0.12\textwidth}X p{0.15\textwidth}p{0.20\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Requirement} & \textbf{Priority} & \textbf{Source} \\
\midrule
FR-01 & The system shall parse a Litho Markdown document according to the three-tier dialect and produce a valid AST. & Must & Language spec \\
FR-02 & The system shall bind parsed content to a selected template's schema and generate compilable \texttt{.tex} source. & Must & Interpreter/compiler \\
FR-03 & The system shall compile generated \texttt{.tex} to PDF using the Tectonic engine. & Must & Build pipeline \\
FR-04 & The system shall embed the original Markdown source, template identifier, and version into the compiled PDF's metadata. & Must & Round-trip fidelity \\
FR-05 & The CLI shall support \texttt{init}, \texttt{build}, \texttt{watch}, and \texttt{extract-source} commands. & Must & Author tooling \\
FR-06 & The CLI shall support publisher commands \texttt{template create/validate/submit}. & Must & Publisher tooling \\
FR-07 & The VS Code extension shall provide syntax highlighting, snippets, and schema-aware IntelliSense for core and template shortcodes. & Must & Editor tooling \\
FR-09 & The marketplace shall allow browsing and viewing listed templates, and support template submission through a GitHub issue that maintainers review and label for listing. & Must & Marketplace \\
FR-10 & The conversion web app shall provide in-browser Markdown-to-PDF conversion with template selection and PDF-to-source restoration. & Must & Conversion web app \\
FR-11 & The conversion web app shall support AI-assisted template authoring and usage grounded in the curated dataset and each template's JSON manifest, with a product-level \texttt{SKILLS.md}. & Must & AI authoring \\
FR-12 & The MCP server shall expose \texttt{build}, \texttt{validate}, \texttt{template}, and \texttt{extract-source} operations to AI assistants. & Must & MCP integration \\
FR-13 & The hosted conversion API shall provide a webhook-triggerable export-to-PDF action and support PDF-to-Markdown source restoration. & Must & API integration \\
FR-14 & The system shall provide flagship templates (university thesis/proposal, IEEE paper, technical book) and support unified organization house templates. & Must & Templates \\
FR-15 & The documentation website shall document installation, configuration, and usage for the CLI, VS Code extension, conversion web app, MCP server, and hosted API. & Must & Documentation \\
FR-16 & All conversion channels (CLI, VS Code extension, conversion web app, MCP server, and hosted API) shall invoke the same interpreter and template-binding pipeline. & Must & Cross-cutting \\
FR-17 & The marketplace shall record and display the number of hearts and downloads for each listed template. & Must & Marketplace \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\section{Non-Functional Requirements \applicability{Development, Hybrid, Game; Research Software Prototype}}
\begin{guidance}
State measurable quality requirements and operational constraints. Replace vague
terms such as \emph{fast}, \emph{secure}, and \emph{user-friendly} with measurable
targets under stated conditions. Include only attributes relevant to the project.
\end{guidance}

\centering

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{0.12\textwidth}p{0.18\textwidth}X p{0.15\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Attribute} & \textbf{Measurable requirement} & \textbf{Priority} \\
\midrule
NFR-01 & Performance & Local Markdown-to-PDF compilation for a typical proposal document (10--15 pages) shall complete in under 10 seconds on a mid-range machine (using Tectonic's self-contained build). & Must \\
NFR-02 & Portability & The CLI shall run on Linux, macOS, and Windows without requiring a full TeX distribution. & Must \\
NFR-03 & Usability & Authors unfamiliar with LaTeX shall be able to produce a formatted document using a flagship template with no direct LaTeX editing required. & Must \\
NFR-04 & Extensibility & New templates shall be added without modifying the core interpreter grammar; extensions shall be declared via template schema/manifests. & Must \\
NFR-05 & Integrity/Fidelity & PDF source extraction shall recover the exact original Markdown source byte-for-byte (metadata round-trip) with no reconstruction loss. & Must \\
NFR-06 & Security & Template compilation in the web/API context shall be sandboxed; API endpoints shall be rate-limited; templates shall be restricted to a declarative schema plus vetted macro packages. & Must \\
NFR-07 & Maintainability & Changes shall be validated by automated tests (grammar/interpreter, metadata round-trip, golden-file PDF regression per flagship template) in CI. & Must \\
NFR-08 & Discoverability & Templates shall be versioned and discoverable via the marketplace with metadata and usage documentation. & Should \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
